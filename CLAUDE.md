# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for working in this repo.

## What this is

`ergodic` is a Causal AI library (from ergodic.ai), published on PyPI as `ergodic`. Three pillars: causal discovery, causal inference, and process intelligence. They all sit on two shared objects, which are built first.

## Core objects (built)

- `ergodic.graph`: a mixed-graph family. One `MixedGraph` core with typed endpoint marks (tail, arrowhead, circle) covers DAG, ADMG, MAG, CPDAG, and PAG. Build with `dag`, `admg`, `cpdag`, `pag`, or `mixed`. Edges read as glyph strings, e.g. `dag(["X -> Y", "Y -> Z"])`. Graphs are immutable value objects: every edit returns a new graph (`set_mark` edits one endpoint). One edge per node pair: conflicting duplicates raise, so a bow is written as an explicit latent confounder (`latent=`). Separation (`m_separated`, `d_separated`) validates node names and reads circle marks through Zhang's definite-status paths (class-invariant on a complete PAG); a CPDAG-kind graph gets the same class reading (undirected edges recode as circles; a mixed-kind graph walks tails literally); surgery (`do`, subgraphs) and interop (networkx, adjacency, dict) live here. The class machinery is public and the validators are real: Meek + Dor-Tarsi (`meek_closure`, `cpdag_of_dag`, `consistent_extension`) make `is_cpdag` complete, and the PAG toolkit (`graph/_pag`: `mag_of_dag` latent projection, `pag_of_mag` canonical PAG, `mag_of_pag` member extraction, `fci_closure` rules R1-R4+R8-R10) makes `is_pag` complete by round trip. Class reachability and certificates ride along: `possible_descendants`/`possible_ancestors` (possibly directed reachability, equals union-over-members in tests), `visible_edges` (Zhang's no-hidden-confounding certificate), and the member enumerators `dags_of_cpdag` (branch + Meek-close, every leaf verified by `cpdag_of_dag`) and `mags_of_pag` (circle assignments filtered by `is_mag` + `pag_of_mag` round trip), both deterministic with a `max_members` cap that raises. No selection bias anywhere yet (R5-R7 not built; selection inputs raise).
- `ergodic.knowledge`: `DomainKnowledge`, prior constraints (required/forbidden edges, tiers, roots, sinks, confounded pairs). Immutable, composable with `&`, self-checking via `conflicts`/`validate`, with projections (`permits_edge`, `is_consistent`, `apply_to`, `edge_mask`) for discovery to read. Adjacency bans cover every edge type and precedence is transitively closed.
- `ergodic.identification`: graph to estimand. `identify_effect(graph, X, Y)` tries adjustment (the complete criterion), front-door, then instruments, and returns an `Identification` record with the estimand. Also `adjustment_set`, `minimal_adjustment_set`, `frontdoor_set`, `instruments`, and their `is_*` checks. Graph-only and deterministic. The graph's face dispatches: DAG/ADMG reason about one world; CPDAG/MAG/PAG run the generalized adjustment criterion (Perkovic 2018: amenability via visible first edges, possible-ancestry forbidden set, definite-status blocking in the proper back-door graph), so a success holds in every member of the class, held executably to all-members enumeration in `test_identification_classes.py`. Class null = no possibly causal path in any member; front-door/instruments raise on a class with a pointer at the enumerators (the IDA pattern); mixed kinds raise. Latent nodes are excluded from every returned set, inputs are validated, instruments are never descendants of the treatment (IV identification is linear/parametric and says so), and a graph with no directed path from treatment to outcome identifies as the zero-effect `"null"` strategy.
- `ergodic.data`: the typed datasets the algorithms read. One `Dataset` base carries a column `Schema` (continuous, categorical, ordinal, datetime, excluded; binary is derived) across five shapes: `TabularData`, `TimeSeriesData`, `PanelData`, `HierarchicalTSData` (with a `Hierarchy` aggregation tree), and `EventLog`. Build with `tabular`, `timeseries`, `panel`, `hts`, `event_log`. Immutable; a dataframe builds it and stays at the boundary (`data/_frame.py`), the math runs in numpy. Accessors: `variables`, `codes`, `matrix`, `dropna`, `select`, `aligns_with(graph)`. Containers only; the per-shape algorithms ride with their pillars.
- `ergodic.inference`: estimation (E1 + E2 built, plus Bayesian learners). `estimate_effect(graph, data, X, Y)` identifies the effect then estimates it, returning a distribution-shaped `EffectEstimate` (a point with an analytic or bootstrap standard error, or draws). A `CausalEstimator` is the strategy and a `Learner` is the model in each nuisance slot; the `Learner` is structurally a sklearn estimator, so any sklearn or xgboost model drops in. Adjustment: `OutcomeRegression` (S/T-learner), `IPW`, `Stratification`, `AIPW`, and the cross-fit `DML`, `DRLearner`, `RLearner`, `XLearner`. Front-door: `TwoStageRegression`. Instruments: `TwoStageLeastSquares`, `Wald`, `PLIV`. Heterogeneous effects through `cate(data)` on the CATE estimators. Binary treatment for most, binary or continuous for `DML` and `PLIV`; `treated=` picks the treated level of a non-numeric treatment (implicit coding warns), and the zero-effect `"null"` family lands in `NullEffect`. Native `OLS`, `LogisticRegression`, and `KNN` learners keep it dependency-free. A `BayesianLearner` carries a posterior over its mean (detected by `predict_draws`); a `PyMCLearner` wraps a user-written PyMC model (pymc optional, imported inside the methods). `OutcomeRegression` (g-formula) and `AIPW` (doubly robust, with a Dirichlet Bayesian bootstrap on by default) then return a posterior `EffectEstimate` (`kind="posterior"`) through the same accessors, and the T-learner gives per-unit CATE credible intervals via `cate_draws`/`cate_interval`. A Bayesian slot never bootstraps (that would refit the sampler); with no posterior path it falls back to the analytic or point result. The quasi-experimental designs sit beside the graph path: `did`/`event_study` over `PanelData` (canonical two-period collapse for one adoption date, Callaway-Sant'Anna group-time ATTs for staggered; `comparison="never_treated"|"not_yet_treated"`; static TWFE offered with its Goodman-Bacon caveat, never auto-picked; unit-bootstrap inference; e=-1 is the omitted reference), `synthetic_control` (one treated unit, simplex weights by projected gradient on the pre-period, Abadie placebo rank p, `interval()` is a placebo band and `std_error` stays None), and `interrupted_time_series` over `TimeSeriesData` (segmented regression, level+slope changes, Fourier seasonality, Newey-West SEs). Each returns an `EffectEstimate` subclass (`DiDEffect`, `SyntheticControlEffect`, `ITSEffect`) carrying a `Design` record (the identification claim in words: parallel trends, convex donor span, continuing trend) instead of an `Identification`.

- `ergodic.discovery`: structure learning (D1 + D2 + D3 built). Two ingredient objects. A `CITest` is a class constructed with data (the stateful oracle: encoding per the test's declared form, cached alpha-independent queries): `Linear`, `Discrete`, `Mixed`, `GCM` (any regression `Learner` in the slots, in-sample residual product, catches nonlinear dependence), plus `DSeparationOracle` as ground truth (answers about the observed margin; latents are not query-able). A decomposable `Score` answers `local(node, parents)` in two declared forms (`local_from_arrays` numeric, `local_from_codes` native categorical): `BIC` (Gaussian), `DiscreteBIC`, `BDeu`, `MixedBIC` (degenerate Gaussian, reduces exactly to `BIC` on single-column nodes); all decomposable and score-equivalent, tested executably. Algorithms are thin consumers declaring `ingredient` ("test" or "score"): `PC` (stable skeleton, v-structures, Meek closure, CPDAG with sepsets), `FCI` (the shared stable skeleton, possible-d-sep re-test, circle v-structures, `fci_closure`; PAG out, held executably to `pag_of_mag(mag_of_dag(truth))` under the oracle on random latent DAGs and ADMGs; knowledge via ancestry: tiers pin an arrowhead at the later end, `confound` orients `<->`, required orients fully, directional forbids/roots/sinks validate only; knowledge-seeded outputs drop to the mixed face with `pag_valid=0.0`), and `MMPC` (max-min parents-children screen, AND rule, undirected skeleton for `restrict_to`; MMHC = MMPC + a score search as a composition) wrap a test; `GES` (Chickering two-phase, CPDAG out, held to the A* optimum in tests), `HillClimb` (steepest ascent with a tabu ring, best-seen state), and `AStar` (exact, order lattice, node cap) wrap a score, resolved at fit (`score="auto"` reads the schema like `test="auto"`). `discover(data, method=...)` is the facade; `bootstrap(data, method=..., n_bootstrap=...)` reruns any method on `Dataset.take` row resamples and returns adjacency/glyph frequencies with a thresholded `summary()` (the first soft output; not posteriors). Knowledge enters in four modes (prune, protect, orient, validate); `DomainKnowledge.restrict_to(skeleton)` confines any search to an adjacency support (a pruned pair records no sepset, so PC-inside-a-screen orients less; score searches lose nothing). Temporal discovery (D-TS): `discover(data, method="pcmci", max_lag=...)` reads `TimeSeriesData` or `PanelData` (windows pooled within entities) through `lagged_tabular` (so any `CITest` and `test="auto"` work unchanged) and returns a `TimeSeriesDiscoveryResult`: a window graph over `X[t-1]`-style nodes (dag kind when fully oriented, mixed otherwise), per-link p-values in `links[(src, dst, lag)]`, `summary_edges()` at the variable level (no summary graph on purpose: cross-lag feedback is normal and one-edge-per-pair is not bent for it). PCMCI shape: PC1 lagged screen, MCI tests conditioned via a 2x-deep lagged frame (truncation falls back with a diagnostic), PC-stable contemporaneous phase conditioned on lagged parents, collider + within-slice Meek R1/R2 (conflicts stay undirected and are counted). `granger` returns a pair-level table, not a graph (predictive, confounding-blind, the docstring says so). Guards: iid methods reject temporal shapes inside `resolve_test`/`resolve_score` (so direct class use is covered), `pcmci` rejects tabular, `bootstrap` refuses temporal methods (moving-block is future work), and knowledge on `pcmci` raises for now.

- `ergodic.process`: process intelligence over an `EventLog` (P1 + P2 built). Descriptive layer: `process_map` (the DFG as a frozen value object, START/END boundary arcs, gap stats in seconds, `simplify(keep=)` records `dropped_arcs`/`dropped_share` and accumulates; deliberately NOT a MixedGraph, A->B and B->A both happen), `performance` (cycle times; waiting/service split only when `lifecycle=` names a column with start/complete pairs, `n_unmatched` counted; complete-only logs honestly cannot split), `variant_table`, and six filters (time with contained/intersecting/trim, variants, endpoints, activities with keep/mandatory/forbidden, follows with within=, performance), each docstring leading with "Drops cases:" or "Drops events:", all passing ascending positions to `take`, raising instead of returning an empty log. Causal layer: `case_table(log, treatment, outcome)` encodes one row per case; treatment = an activity name or a case-constant 0/1 column; outcome = "cycle_time"/"n_events" (reserved, collision raises) or a column; `when="anytime"` uses case-start features only (leak-free by construction), `("after_events", k)`/`("after_elapsed", x)` are landmark designs (prefix features at the landmark, already-treated dropped into `n_dropped_treated`, short cases into `n_dropped_short`, estimand population = cases reaching the landmark untreated); named confounders ride along (workload via `open_cases`, calendar on datetime stamps); auto `DomainKnowledge` tiers [covariates] < [treatment] < [outcome] forbid post-treatment adjustment mechanically; zero-variance derived covariates drop silently. `kpi_panel(log, period, unit=, kpis=)` aggregates cases by start-bucket into a `PanelData` (or `TimeSeriesData` when unit=None) for the panel designs; periods are day/week/month on datetimes or a float width on numeric stamps; derived kpi names are reserved. Shared case arithmetic in `process/_cases.py` (case_slices, case_bounds, case_attribute, open_cases, time_seconds). The composed pipeline is the anchor test: complexity world where the naive review gap is 10.8 against a planted 5.0 and case_table -> discover -> estimate lands at 5.05; rollout world where kpi_panel -> did recovers -3.0 within 0.04. PC + tier knowledge returns the mixed face, so the composed route re-faces with `as_kind("dag")` (the guide-06 pattern).

These re-export from the top level: `from ergodic import dag, admg, DomainKnowledge, identify_effect, tabular, estimate_effect, discover, did, event_study, synthetic_control, interrupted_time_series, granger, case_table, kpi_panel, process_map`.

## Design decisions to keep

- Full graph surface from day one (marks model), algorithms added in phases.
- Immutable value objects everywhere (graphs and knowledge).
- Hard domain knowledge now. `energy`/`log_prior` are stubbed and raise; soft/Bayesian knowledge attaches there later.
- networkx only at the interop boundary in `graph/_io.py`. The algorithms are native.
- No graph posteriors yet; revisit at the Bayesian phase.
- Data enters as a typed `Dataset`. The dataframe library lives only at `data/_frame.py` (pandas native, polars and arrow via `to_pandas`; narwhals deferred). The two axes, row shape and column schema, stay separate.
- Estimation factors into a `CausalEstimator` (the strategy) and a `Learner` (the sklearn estimator surface, native by default, sklearn/xgboost optional). The result is distribution-shaped: a point now, posterior draws later through the same `EffectEstimate`.
- Categorical encoding belongs to the algorithm wrapper, not the data layer. `data.matrix()` stays integer codes; estimation one-hots in its own design builder (`_covariate_matrix`); a discovery conditional-independence test owns its strategy, one-hot or natively categorical.

## Working here

- uv for everything: `uv sync --group docs` to set up.
- `make check` runs ruff (lint and format check), mypy strict, and pytest. `make docs-serve` previews the docs; `make docs` builds strict.
- mypy is strict and ruff is the linter and formatter. Keep both green.
- `make docs` executes the learn notebooks, so it is also their test: a cell that raises fails the build. Re-verify it after touching `docs/learn/` or anything they call. Numbers quoted in their prose must match the seeded outputs; rerun and recalibrate the prose when a simulation changes.
- `make docs-deploy` ships the docs to Cloud Run (service `ergodic-docs`, GCP project `ergodic`, us-central1) via `deploy/docs/deploy.sh`: local amd64 docker build, Artifact Registry push, deploy by image. Needs a fresh `gcloud auth login` for andre@ergodic.ai; the script header records the one-time setup and why the source-deploy path is avoided.
- Prose in `plans/` and `docs/` follows `plans/guides/writing_guide.md` (no em-dashes, plain words, contractions; user-facing docs in the Witten register the guide describes).

## What's next

Built object-first: graph, knowledge, identification (single graphs and equivalence classes), the typed data family, estimation through E2 plus Bayesian learners and the quasi-experimental designs (DiD with staggered group-time, event studies, synthetic control, interrupted time series), and discovery D1 + D2 + D3 plus temporal discovery (PC, FCI, MMPC, GES, HillClimb, A*, PCMCI window graphs, Granger; the Gaussian, discrete, and mixed scores; the GCM learner test; the bootstrap stability wrapper). The discover-identify-estimate bridge is closed: `estimate_effect` runs straight off a CPDAG or PAG through the generalized adjustment criterion.

The process pillar's P1 + P2 are built (the design lives in `plans/*-process-intelligence-design.md`): the map and the case table, and the causal bridges into discovery, estimation, and the panel designs. Still ahead per the design: P3 the model layer (Inductive Miner to sound process trees and workflow nets with a real `is_sound`, token replay then alignments, decision-point CATE at XOR splits) and P4 the frontier (off-policy evaluation of intervention policies on raw logs, effect drift across change points, case-level counterfactuals over a fitted SCM, queueing SCMs, the simulator audit, OCEL object-centric logs). pm4py is AGPL: implement from papers, never depend or port.

Other open tracks: discovery D4 (selection bias R5-R7, RFCI and conservative/majority variants, glasso screen, GRaSP/BOSS, DirectLiNGAM, VAR-LiNGAM/tsFCI/LPCMCI on the temporal side, moving-block bootstrap for temporal methods, knowledge on PCMCI); estimation E3 remainder (TMLE, IV/front-door posteriors, cross-fit Bayesian nuisances, joint PyMC estimators, DiD anticipation windows and predictor-matching synthetic control, multi-unit synthetic control); identification follow-ups (do-calculus ID, class-level front-door/instruments, `ida()`, identification on window graphs); and `ergodic.datasets` (LaLonde, Sachs; download-on-demand, licensing checked). Plan a phase before writing it, and update the journal as you go.
